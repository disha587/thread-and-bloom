Crochet, Made To Your Idea

From soft amigurumi characters to cosy wearables and little home pieces — every crochet order is worked by hand in the yarn, colours and size you choose. Send a reference photo or describe what you're picturing, and I'll bring it to life stitch by stitch.

  • Amigurumi dolls, animals and characters

  • Home decor — coasters, baskets, wall hangings

Custom Embroidery

Embroidery On Your Garments

Names, initials, little illustrations, or a design you already love — hand-stitched onto t-shirts, shirts, hoodies and more. Send your own garment for me to embroider, or ask for a plain tee with your design stitched fresh.

  • Names, initials, quotes and small illustrations
  • On t-shirts, shirts, hoodies, tote bags and caps
  • Send your own garment, or I'll provide a plain one
  • Share your own design or a reference image to recreate
